A few quick answers

Good to know.

How long does a website take?

A focused small-business website often takes a few weeks from kickoff to launch. The schedule depends on the number of pages and how quickly we can gather content and feedback.

Can you work with my existing brand?

Absolutely. I can work with the logo, colors, and materials you already have, or help refine the visual direction as part of the project.

Will I be able to update it myself?

Yes. We’ll agree on an approach that fits your site, and I’ll walk you through the parts you’ll want to change regularly.
